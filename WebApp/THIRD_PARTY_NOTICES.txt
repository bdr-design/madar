## lz4js 0.2.0 block compressor

`WebApp/save-chunk-codec.js` includes the block compressor and `readU32` / `hashU32` helpers from [Benzinga/lz4js](https://github.com/Benzinga/lz4js), npm `lz4js@0.2.0`, licensed ISC. The local wrapper bounds input/output, shuffles exact bytes, adds CRC-32, and uses a separate checked decoder. The upstream frame reader and its unbounded allocation path are not included.
